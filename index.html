This Privacy Policy explains how Bao Kien Tran, the individual developer of Trim ("we", "us", "the app"), collects, uses, and protects your information when you use the Trim mobile application. By using Trim, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Who we are

Trim is a weight-management application operated by Bao Kien Tran, an individual developer based in Ontario, Canada. You can contact us at trim.app.help@gmail.com regarding any privacy question or request.

2. Information we collect

We collect only what the app needs to function:

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, or any information the app does not need. We do not sell your data to anyone.

3. How we use your information

We use your information solely to: provide the app's features (calorie tracking, TDEE estimation, progress statistics); operate AI-assisted food and activity analysis; secure your account and prevent abuse; and, if you opt in, notify you about your progress. We do not use your health data for advertising.

4. Third-party services and AI

To provide certain features, Trim shares limited data with the following service providers:

  • OpenAI — When you use AI food or activity analysis, the text you write and any food photo you capture are sent to OpenAI's API to estimate nutrition and activity. OpenAI processes this data to return a result and, per its API terms, does not use API data to train its models. This may involve transfer to servers in the United States. AI analysis is optional — you can log all food, activity, and weight manually without using it.
  • MongoDB Atlas — Hosts the database that stores your account and logs.
  • Apple — Processes any in-app purchases; we never receive your payment card details.

5. AI results are estimates

Calorie, macronutrient, and activity figures produced by AI are estimates and may be inaccurate. They are not medical or nutritional advice. Please see our Terms of Service for the full health disclaimer.

6. Data retention and deletion

We keep your data while your account is active. You can permanently delete your account and all associated data at any time from within the app (Settings → Delete Account). Deletion is immediate and irreversible. If you contact us to delete your data, we will do so within 30 days.

7. How we protect your data

Passwords are stored using industry-standard one-way hashing and are never stored in plain text. Access to the app requires authentication tokens, which are stored securely on your device and rotated regularly. We restrict access to personal data to what is necessary to operate the service.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data, and to withdraw consent. Canadian users are protected under PIPEDA; EU/UK users under the GDPR; California users under the CCPA. To exercise any right, contact trim.app.help@gmail.com. We will not discriminate against you for exercising these rights.

9. Children

Trim is not intended for anyone under 17. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 17. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

10. International users

Because some of our providers (such as OpenAI and our database host) operate in the United States, your information may be processed outside your country. By using Trim you consent to this transfer.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. Material changes will be announced in the app. Continued use after a change means you accept the updated policy.
